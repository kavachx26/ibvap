# Sample footage

These clips appear under **Video Sources** in the dashboard (locally and on the hosted demo) and run through the full detection, tracking, behaviour and ANPR pipeline.

| File | Scene |
|---|---|
| `kolkata_street_traffic.mp4` | Kolkata street: taxi, vans, cars, pedestrians |
| `coastal_road_traffic.mp4` | Coastal road: moving and parked cars |
| `city_crossing_crowd.mp4` | Busy city crossing: dense crowd and buses |
| `pedestrian_street.mp4` | Pedestrian street with seated groups |

They are reduced copies (960 px wide, 15 fps, no audio) of third-party stock footage, included only so the demo works out of the box. They are sized for small cloud CPUs; drop any higher-resolution MP4, AVI, MOV or MKV here to analyse it at full quality.
